3

Get your Oriya Index

Your raw score converts the instant it's read — Whoop 80 → Index 73 — same math for every device. The Index isn't your raw number: it's where this morning sits in your own ~90-day range (floor → par → ceiling), so your device's quirks cancel out and every body competes fairly. A person only checks that the screenshot is a real capture — it posts within the hour and never changes your number.

First ~14 days, a clearly-labelled per-device estimate stands in while your baseline builds. Full math + published factors: the science · why we refuse raw-number boards: the manifesto.

Quick answers

Connect my device? No — a screenshot is enough. Oura owners get optional live read-only sync.
Miss a morning? Counts 0 that day. You're never removed from the board.
Bad score? A logged red morning outranks a forgotten green one. The board scores the log, not the flex.
Why not just rank raw scores? Raw boards crown genetics and the most generous algorithm. Here your rank is the gap you hold to Par 71, read against your own baseline — the math · the why.
Who sees my data? Name/handle, device, adjusted score. Anon mode shows only your company domain.
Cost? Free either way. If a sponsor backs a pot, backers fund it, athletes win it, backers are never paid out. Payment only via verified Airwallex links from team@oriya.app. It's not a wager.
Getting paid? No sponsor on the day-to-day board yet, so nothing to pay out there. The Oriya Olympics bracket carries a guaranteed prize pool of at least $2,000 for the season's highest sustained Index.
