Why it's fair

Everyone races their own par — not each other's raw score.

A Whoop 83 and an Oura 82 don't mean the same thing, and a genetically-gifted 90 shouldn't beat your hard-won 74. So the Network never ranks raw numbers. Ori learns your own 90-day baseline, then ranks you by how far over your own par you landed today. The person who improved most against themselves wins — whatever they wear, whatever they were born with.

01 · Your line

Calibrated to you

Floor, par, ceiling — read from your own history across Oura, Whoop, Garmin, and Apple Health. A 61 on your worst-sleep week isn't a 61 on your best.
