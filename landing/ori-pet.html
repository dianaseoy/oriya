Apple Silicon (M-series) · ~2.8 MB · unsigned early build.

First time opening Ori? (30 seconds)
  1. Open the .dmg, drag Ori into Applications.
  2. Double-click Ori. macOS says it “can’t be checked for malicious software.” That’s expected — it’s an unsigned early build, not broken. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click “Open Anyway” next to Ori, confirm — then Ori opens every morning with a normal double-click.
