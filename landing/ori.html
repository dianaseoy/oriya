What brings you here?

Pick the pain — Ori tunes to it. Change it anytime.

Ori v0.1 is an early AI companion — not a doctor, and it can be wrong. It'll nudge you toward an easier day, never give medical advice. Worth knowing: your messages and Ori's replies pass through Braintrust, a tracing tool a solo founder uses to see how Ori's doing and fix it — never sold, no accounts, no database. A screenshot you share isn't kept, but the reply Ori writes about it is traced like any other message — posting to the board still happens at oriya.app/submit. Voice typing uses your browser's built-in speech service. Built in the open by a solo founder.
